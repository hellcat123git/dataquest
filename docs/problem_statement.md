# DATAQUEST 3.0: DQNM Problem Statement Analysis

## Title
**PRISM Engine:** Multi-Dimensional STEAM Career Guidance & Hyper-Local Innovation Platform

## Background
In India, over 90% of school and college students lack structured, evidence-based career guidance. Critical decisions are often made without understanding:
*   Market demand for specific professions.
*   Real-world salary trajectories.
*   Alternate interdisciplinary pathways.
*   Accessible scholarships.

Furthermore, parents (the primary financial and emotional decision-makers) are routinely excluded from the analytical evaluation process. This leads to severe family conflict and financially unsustainable career commitments.

**Nano Mech Labs** builds innovation-based STEAM education platforms to nurture hyper-local marketable solutions from school ecosystems. 
The **PRISM Engine** will bridge the existing gap by mathematically evaluating student psychometrics, parental financial vectors, and evolving global market opportunities.

## The Problem
Students, parents, and educators navigating career planning often suffer from several systemic shortcomings:
1.  **Geographic Demand Opacity:** Unawareness of specific regions and global markets where specialized skills are most valued.
2.  **Economic Yield Mismatch:** Lack of clarity regarding actual compensation trajectories and Return on Investment (ROI) following course completion.
3.  **Parental Exclusion & Financial Blindspots:** Failure to systematically analyze household affordability, leading to debt or misaligned expectations.
4.  **Unexplored Career Alternatives:** Rigid conventional career choices that overlook emerging hyper-local and STEAM innovation pathways.
5.  **Disconnected Financial Aid Discovery:** Lack of timely visibility into relevant entrance examinations, institutional rankings, and targeted scholarships.

*Result:* High career dropout rates, unfulfilled aspirations, and graduate underemployment.

## The Challenge
Design and develop the **PRISM Engine** to assist students and families in exploring viable career pathways through multi-factor evaluation. The system must satisfy both:
1.  **Multi-dimensional Assessment:** Evaluating student competencies and family constraints.
2.  **Dynamic Alignment:** Mapping capabilities with dynamic industry opportunities and educational pathways.

### Evaluation Scope
*   **Student Profile:** Assessment of aptitude, cognitive preferences, and interest indicators.
*   **Family Profile:** Consideration of financial constraints, risk appetite, and family considerations.
*   **Market Mapping:** Mapping profile factors against evolving sector demand and career options.

### Target Guidance Scenario
When assessing a student's profile for career planning, the engine should:
*   Combine student evaluation metrics and family parameters into composite scores.
*   Identify suitable career domains and highlight potential alignment gaps.
*   Recommend relevant educational pathways, examinations, and development options.

## Core Concepts & Pipeline
The PRISM workflow executes a structured multi-vector analysis pipeline:

1.  **Ingest Multi-Stakeholder Inputs:**
    *   Collect psychometric assessment responses from students.
    *   Collect financial and aspirational parameters from parents.
2.  **Vectorize, Normalize, and Solve Constraints:**
    *   Apply mathematical normalization.
    *   Run the Financial Constraint Solver.
    *   Compute the **Parent-Student Conflict Index**.
3.  **Synthesize Market Intelligence & Generate Roadmap:**
    *   Blend normalized vectors with dynamic industry hiring indices.
    *   Output optimized, financially viable career recommendations.
